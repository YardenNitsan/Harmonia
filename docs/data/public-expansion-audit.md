# Public corpus expansion audit

Audit date: 2026-09-20. Engineering acquisition decisions, not a warranty of worldwide rights or product accuracy. Primary publisher records, embedded notices and installed audited model code were inspected. No streaming-service capture is proposed. GuitarSet family-3 has already been evaluated and remains unavailable for tuning, selection or training.

## Decision

The next bounded acquisition is **Winterreise HU33 songs 02 and 18**, approximately 4.95 MB compressed audio, with their matching annotations. These are real voice-and-piano recordings with harmonic annotations. The embedded HU33 notice declares Public Domain Mark 1.0; this is materially different from SC06's embedded noncommercial/no-derivatives license. Acquire no SC06 audio. A successful pilot would justify considering the remaining HU33 material, not claiming general-song accuracy.

The composition split is fixed before any new training: HU33 02–13 training, 14–18 validation, 19–24 locked test. Exclude 01 because the main release inserts an artificial repeat. Song 18 annotations were inspected during this audit and cannot be test data. Group all performances, excerpts, augmentations and alternate edits of a composition together. Do not evaluate 19–24 until a new selection is frozen. These adjacent-number splits are small, single-cycle samples, not independent composer/performer/genre splits.

### Annotation hazards observed before acquisition

- CSV uses semicolons with `start;end;shorthand;extended;majmin;majmin_inv` fields. Preserve every original field and timing string.
- Slash bass is an **absolute pitch spelling**, e.g. `A:7/C#` and `G:min/A#`, not a Harte relative degree. Convert to `A:7/3` and `G:min/#2` before existing label handling. Preserve diatonic spelling, including double accidentals; the latter is enharmonically equivalent to a minor third but not spelled as one.
- Explicit degrees occur, e.g. `G#:(b3,b5,bb7)/B`. Existing reduced-model encoding is not a lossless canonical label representation.
- In song 18, 10.94–13.04 seconds, shorthand `A:(3,5,b7,b9)` conflicts with extended `A:(3,5,b9)`. Detect disagreement after semantic expansion, preserve both, and exclude conflicts from future strict structural targets until reviewed; do not silently choose one as ground truth.
- Main song 01 contains an inserted repeat. Matching original-short material is a distinct variant, not an extra independent composition. It is outside this pilot/split.

Before full acquisition: validate WAV integrity, duration/annotation range, overlap/gaps, bass conversion and conflict counts on the pilot. Record silence/unannotated coverage separately. Do not fit normalization or evaluate models as part of this acquisition check.

## Stronger existing model path

LV-Chordia remains the strongest measured local baseline on the existing representative validation subset, not a shipped app recognizer. See [evaluation](../evaluation.md) for aligned comparisons. The [original authors' repository](https://github.com/music-x-lab/ISMIR2019-Large-Vocabulary-Chord-Recognition) and [packaging repository](https://github.com/openmirlab/lv-chordia) both provide MIT notices covering the published code/artifact distribution; retain them and hashes. This does not grant rights to its historic training recordings.

Installed version 1.1.0 was inspected read-only. A concrete conversion path is to export each of five networks' six raw-logit heads with fixed batch 1 and dynamic frame count, compare CPU PyTorch/ONNX probabilities, average **probabilities** across models, and preserve the original HMM decoder. The five installed checkpoint files total 28,730,939 bytes; converted size and speed are unmeasured.

Preprocessing is magnitude hybrid CQT, 22,050 Hz, hop 512, 36 bins/octave, F#0 minimum, 288 bins, automatic tuning; network input crops columns 18:270. It cannot consume Harmonia's 26-feature chroma contract. Sequence-wide instance normalization and bidirectional recurrence mean arbitrary chunks or the small TCN overlap do not preserve predictions. CQT/tuning/resampling, ensemble arithmetic and NumPy HMM decoding all need parity fixtures and measured bounded-runtime design. No conversion or new quality gain is claimed by this audit. This is a stronger implementation lead while the new real-audio pilot is checked; E004 remains frozen and experimental, DSP remains default.
